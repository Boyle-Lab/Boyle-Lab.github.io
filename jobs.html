Student opportunities

Before contacting the lab

Thank you for your interest in our research. Please review the guidance below before contacting me about student research opportunities. Eligibility and application procedures differ by student level.

Prospective PhD students

Students applying from outside the University of Michigan should apply to a graduate program through the University, generally through the Program in Biomedical Sciences (PIBS). Admission is determined through the program’s application and review process. I can not offer PhD admission through email inquiries.

My lab is affiliated with the following PIBS programs: Bioinformatics, Genetics and Genomics, and Cellular and Molecular Biology. Students admitted through these programs may rotate in or later join the lab, depending on research interests and mentoring capacity.

You do not need to contact me or obtain my approval before applying through PIBS or mentioning my name in your application.

If you are invited to interview, I would be glad to discuss your research interests and answer questions about the lab at that stage.

Bioinformatics PhD admissions

Current University of Michigan graduate students

We are currently accepting Ph.D. students. Rotation positions are available.

Undergraduate students

Undergraduate research opportunities are limited to students currently enrolled at the University of Michigan who can participate in person in Ann Arbor. We do not consider research-placement requests from undergraduates at other institutions, including requests for summer visits or remote projects.

Eligible students should include their major, year, relevant coursework or experience, weekly availability, and the semesters they could commit to research in an email to me. Opportunities depend on available projects and mentoring capacity.

High-school students

High-school research opportunities, when advertised, are limited to students attending school in the Ann Arbor area who can participate in person. Please contact the lab only in response to a posted opportunity and follow its application instructions. We do not consider unsolicited requests for individual mentorship, remote projects, or placements from outside the area.

Writing your inquiry

For eligible inquiries, a short message is sufficient. Describe your own interests, relevant experience, and what you are seeking. Please do not send mass-distributed messages or use automated agents to contact the lab on your behalf.

I cannot respond individually to requests that fall outside these guidelines or to questions answered on this page.
