MapGL, our new tool for simplification of phylogenetic inference for the evolutionary history of short sequence elements, is published in BMC Bioinformatics. MapGL packages reference mapping and phylogenetic reconstruction into a streamlined workflow for inferring whether short genomic features were gained or lost during evolution. It makes evolutionary analysis of regulatory sequences accessible to researchers without requiring complex custom pipelines. Congratulations to Adam on leading this work!
