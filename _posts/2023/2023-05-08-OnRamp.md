Our OnRamp paper is published in Genome Research! OnRamp combines multiplexed nanopore sequencing with an automated analysis workflow for rapid, full-length plasmid validation. The method identifies point mutations and structural discrepancies across many plasmids at once, offering an accessible way to improve the quality and reproducibility of plasmid-based experiments. Congratulations to Camille and Melissa on leading this work!
