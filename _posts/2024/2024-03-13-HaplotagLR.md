Our method HaplotagLR is published in PLOS ONE! HaplotagLR assigns long sequencing reads to parental haplotypes using phased variants and a configurable statistical error model. It outperformed the leading method in simulations and recovered more haplotagged reads from real nanopore data while giving users direct control over the sensitivity–specificity tradeoff. Congratulations to Monica on leading this work!
