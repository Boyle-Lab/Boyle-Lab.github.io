The Boyle Lab celebrated a huge milestone; the lab's 10-year anniversary! We kicked off the day with research lightning talks from current lab members and a game of lab-trivia. We also had a panel of lab alumni who joined both via Zoom and in person to share updates on their careers and reflect on their previous time in the lab. Afterward, the group enjoyed lunch together at a local Chinese restaurant, and then tested their teamwork at an escape room. The lab wrapped up the day with an evening celebration at Alan's house, with fun games and cake. It was a wonderful opportunity to celebrate a decade of research, collaboration, and the many people who have been part of the Boyle Lab over the past 10 years.
