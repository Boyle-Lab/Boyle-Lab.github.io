F-seq 2, our long-awaited update to the F-seq peak caller, is published in NAR Genomics and Bioinformatics. F-Seq2 modernizes feature-density peak calling by combining kernel density estimation with a dynamic Poisson test that adapts to local sequencing biases. The result is a faster, more accurate way to identify and rank enriched regions in functional genomics data. Congratulations to Nanxiang on leading this work!
